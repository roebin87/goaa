# Mechanism · Wrap-Up (Five Actions)
> **Semantics**: Machine (mechanism/translation layer · How)

> Five save actions that must complete before ending a conversation — **the AI has no memory; if it's not persisted, it's gone**.

## The Five Actions

| # | Action | Target | Note |
|---|------|------|------|
| 1 | Dialogue record | `_Memory/dialogue-record.md` | verbatim record of this session (append · never edit/delete) |
| 2 | Distillation update | `_Memory/distill.md` | condense this session's essence (overwrite with latest) |
| 3 | Daily summary | `_Memory/daily-summary.md` | what was done today (append) |
| 4 | Your sayings | `_Memory/your-sayings.md` | your quotes / key views (by topic) |
| 5 | Wrap-up confirmation | verbal report | "Memory persisted: N new items today" |

## Iron Rules

- **No dialogue record persisted = no wrap-up** — the verbatim record must be completed before ending
- **Memory files are append-only, never edited/deleted** — your history stays forever

## After the First Wrap-Up

Next time you open a new conversation, the AI reads the distillation → continuation succeeds = you now have an AI that "remembers you". This is what distinguishes GOAA from ordinary AI: **memory stays in your hands, never lost across days**.
